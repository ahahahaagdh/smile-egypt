Beyond the appointment

A smoother journey to Egypt

Planning treatment abroad involves more than choosing a procedure. Ask your clinic about the number of visits, recovery time, follow-up care, and what happens if plans change.

  • Understand the proposed treatment and timeline
  • Ask for an itemised written estimate
  • Discuss travel dates only after clinical guidance
  • Plan accommodation and local transport independently until partners are confirmed
